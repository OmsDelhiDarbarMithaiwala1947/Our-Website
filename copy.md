# Om's Dehlii Darbar Mithaiwala — copy deck
### Rakshabandhan 2026 · Shop No. 20, Shivaji Nagar, Delisle Road, Lower Parel (E), Mumbai — 400 013

Everything here is already set into the brochure and the website. This is the master
text: reuse it, hand it to a printer, or re-dress it for Diwali in October.

**Voice.** Taken from the shop's own printed letter, not invented. Warm, familial,
confident, a little formal. It opens "Dear patron," and it addresses a family and a
company in the same breath, because the shop has always sold to both. Its slight
non-standard grammar is authentic to a 79-year-old family business and has been kept.

**Banned throughout:** artisanal, curated, elevate, indulge, delve, unleash, bespoke,
"nestled in the heart of". No health or nutrition claims. "Sugar Free", "Khajur
Sugarfree" and "Anjeer Sugarfree" are used only as the product names the menu prints.

**Spellings preserved exactly as printed:** Dehlii · Katri · Burfi *and* Barfi (the
menu uses both — each is kept as printed) · Maysore Pak · Mahim Halwa · Kalingar ·

## 1. Hero headline + subhead — three options

### Option A — **recommended**

> **Made the same way since 1947.**
> रक्षाबंधन की मिठास
> *All varieties of dry fruit, milk, Bengali and mawa mithais, manufactured by us. Retail and corporate orders. Fancy packaging available.*

**Why this one.** It leads with the one thing no competitor can copy — seventy-nine
years on the same road — and it is a claim about method, not about taste, so it is
defensible. It carries the owner's own description underneath almost word for word.
And it still works on 29 August, which matters: you are not rewriting the home page
the day after Rakhi.

### Option B — festival-forward

> **Celebrate the Sweetest Bandhan**
> रक्षाबंधन की मिठास
> *Fancy Rakhi Gift Hampers from ₹1,400. Made in our own kitchen on Delisle Road since 1947. Order on WhatsApp.*

Stronger for the fortnight before 28 August, weaker for the other fifty weeks.
**This is the headline used on the Rakhi Specials page**, which is where it belongs.

### Option C — neighbourhood-forward

> **Lower Parel's mithai shop since 1947.**
> लोअर परेल की अपनी मिठाई की दुकान
> *Minutes from the station, on Delisle Road. Mithai, dry fruits, farsan and chocolates, made here.*

Best for search — it contains the phrase people actually type. Slightly flatter to
read, so it is used as the home page **title tag** rather than the on-page headline.

## 2. The "Dear patron," letter

### As printed on the menu (Dussehra / Diwali) — kept for the October re-dress

> **Dear patron,**
> You must be looking forward to yet another prosperous and festive season of Dussehra
> leading into Diwali win over your friends & business associates and make business time
> more tastier and sweeter with rich Bengali Mithai, Rasgulla, Ras Malai, Kaju Katri,
> Motichoor Laddu, Badam Pista Burfi & Standard (Mix Mithai) Packs and Dryfruit Chocolates.

### Rewritten for Rakshabandhan — **use this one**

> **Dear patron,**
> You must be looking forward to yet another warm and festive season of Rakshabandhan
> win over your friends & business associates and make the bond more tastier and sweeter
> with rich Bengali Mithai, Rasgulla, Ras Malai, Kaju Katri, Motichoor Laddu, Anjeer
> Katri, Kaju Pista Roll & Standard (Mix Mithai) Packs and Dryfruit Chocolates.

*53 words. One sentence. "Dear patron," verbatim.*

**What was deliberately not corrected.** "win over" still runs on without a comma.
"more tastier and sweeter" is left exactly as the shop writes it. Both were kept
because they are the voice. Cleaning them up would produce something that reads like
every other sweet shop's website, which is the opposite of the point.

**What changed.** Dussehra/Diwali → Rakshabandhan. "make business time" → "make the
bond", because Rakhi is about a bond and the pun is the shop's kind of joke. Badam
Pista Burfi → Anjeer Katri and Kaju Pista Roll, both of which are on the price list at
₹1,400 and ₹1,000 — Badam Pista Burfi is named in the original letter but carries no
rate anywhere, so it is flagged rather than sold.

### 300 words — about page, press, festival features

> Since 1947 one family has made mithai on Delisle Road. The shop was small then, and
> Lower Parel was mills and chawls rather than glass towers. The mills closed. The towers
> came. The shop stayed, at Shop No. 20, Shivaji Nagar, minutes from the station.
>
> The trays change with the season. Mawa through the cold months, when milk sweets keep
> and everybody wants them. Dry fruit through the wedding months, when the orders come in
> by the kilo. From July the counter turns over to Rakhi, and from October to Diwali, and
> then it starts again.
>
> What has not changed is the method. Everything you see is made by us, in our own
> kitchen. Nothing is bought in from a wholesaler and re-labelled. The kaju katri is
> rolled and cut each morning. The tarbuj is built up in coloured layers so it comes out
> of the tray looking like a slice of watermelon. The jamuns sit in syrup until they are
> heavy.
>
> And the milk and mawa sweets are meant to be eaten within twelve hours; the Bengali
> sweets go in the fridge and get eaten within six. We print that on the menu. It is not
> a disclaimer — it is the reason they taste the way they do. There is nothing in them to
> make them last longer, and we are not going to start adding it now.
>
> Three generations of Lower Parel have grown up on this counter. Grandmothers who bought
> here send grandchildren who work in the offices across the road, and those offices order
> sixty hampers at a time with a logo on the box. Both get the same sweets, off the same
> tray. That is not a policy. There is only one tray.
>
> Still on the same road. Still open daily until ten.

## 9. FAQ

**How long do the sweets keep?**
Milk and mawa sweets should be eaten the same day, within twelve hours. Bengali sweets
should go in the fridge and be eaten within six hours. Dry-fruit sweets and the boxed
assortments keep longer. These are the shop's own printed rules, and they are printed
because there is nothing in the sweets to make them last longer.

**Do you deliver?**
Delivery areas and charges: `[[delivery areas and charges]]`. Message us on WhatsApp
with your area and we will confirm before you pay. Kindly check goods during delivery —
we shall not entertain any complaints thereafter.

**Can I have a box made up the way I want it?**
Yes. The four assortment bands — ₹1,400, ₹700, ₹560 and ₹280 a kilo — are the usual
starting point, and the fancy boxes run from a 6 Part at ₹700 up to the Jumbo at ₹3,000.
Tell us a budget and a weight and we will build it. Fancy box charges are extra.

**Is there a discount on large orders?**
Yes — discount is available on bulk orders. Minimum order quantity: `[[MOQ]]`. Lead
time: `[[lead time]]`. For Rakhi and Diwali we fill up early, so call as soon as you
have a number.

**Can you put our company logo on the boxes?**
Send it to us on WhatsApp and we will show you what it looks like before anything is
printed.

**What is in the sweets? I have an allergy.**
They contain dairy — milk, khoya, mawa and ghee — and tree nuts, mostly cashew, almond
and pistachio. Many are finished with edible silver varq. Some items contain wheat.
Everything is made in one kitchen that handles nuts and dairy all day, so cross-contact
cannot be ruled out. Please tell us when you order.

**Can we taste before ordering a large quantity?**
Please call for sampling.

## 11. The WhatsApp order message

Every order button on the site opens WhatsApp with the item and its exact rate already
written out, so the shop never has to ask "which one?".

> Hello Om's Dehlii Darbar! I'd like to order: **\*Anjeer Katri — ₹1,400 per kg\***. Delivery area: \_\_\_\_

An assortment band:

> Hello Om's Dehlii Darbar! I'd like to order: **\*Assorted Dry Fruits Mix Sweets — ₹1,400 per kg\***. Delivery area: \_\_\_\_

A hamper, with the box note carried through:

> Hello Om's Dehlii Darbar! I'd like to order: **\*Fancy Rakhi Gift Hamper, 8 Parts — 1 Kg (₹2,500)\***. I understand fancy box charges are extra. Delivery area: \_\_\_\_

Anything the menu does not price:

> Hello Om's Dehlii Darbar! I'd like to order: **\*Sutarfeni\***. Please tell me the price. Delivery area: \_\_\_\_

The corporate form composes a longer message from what the visitor typed — company,
name, hamper, quantity, date needed, delivery area, notes — and opens WhatsApp with it
laid out on separate lines. Nothing is stored on the website.
